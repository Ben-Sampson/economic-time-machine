## Before Oct 8 (reconstructed from Hunter's Grok chat, `grok_conversation.md`)
- Hunter asked Grok whether Excel could replace Google Sheets. Grok said yes and wrote a long Copilot prompt for an 8-sheet workbook (ReadMe, DataDictionary, RawData, MonthlyPanel, Calculations, Dashboard, Analogs, Sources) built around one question: "What past year does 2026 most resemble?"
- Copilot built the skeleton. Grok's review found the problems: timestamp-style dates, MonthlyPanel only covering 1950–51, nothing linked between sheets, and a dead FRED gold series (`GOLDAMGBD228NLBM`, removed 2022).
- Fixed MonthlyPanel to one row per month, Jan 1950 to Sep 2026.
- CPI (`CPIAUCSL`) pulled from FRED and wired in with XLOOKUP. Hunter had to work through FRED's CSV layout and "######" columns.
- Inflation_12m calculated from CPI.
- Unemployment (`UNRATE`) wired in. A check caught bad readings (Jan 2000 showed 2.8 vs about 4.0 official), then Apr 2020 = 14.8 confirmed it was correct.
- Fed funds (`FEDFUNDS`) wired in. Next step was the Dashboard snapshot boxes, where Copilot "didn't do much" and Grok gave hand-paste LOOKUP formulas.
